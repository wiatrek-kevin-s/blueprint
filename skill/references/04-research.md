# Research

Research runs broadly in phase 2, narrowly in phase 3 (the real options for each one-way door) and phase 4 (each section's details), and once more in phase 5 to re-check what the design rests on.

## Rules

1. Look up anything that can change (versions, prices, limits, licences, laws, who maintains a project), however familiar it seems, because training data is dated.
2. Prefer primary sources (official documentation, specifications, release notes, government sites); use blogs to find them.
3. Open what you cite, because a search snippet is not a source.
4. Write "as of YYYY-MM-DD" beside every current-state fact.
5. Label opinion, inference, vendor benchmarks and single-author claims.
6. Seek the strongest case against each recommendation before adopting it.
7. List what you couldn't verify, and mark it where it is used.
8. Summarize in your own words; quote only short phrases.

## Delegating

Give every research subagent the brief in `references/context-and-agents.md`, its only copy. Split research by topic, never by role, because each hand-off between roles loses detail. Bulky results go to a file such as `research/<topic>.md`, recorded in the state file; bring the person only what affects their decisions. Without subagents, do the same research yourself, one topic at a time.
