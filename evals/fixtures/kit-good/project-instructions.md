# Project instructions: Receipt Sorter

You are building a command-line script that files scanned receipts into a folder for each month, for a person who keeps household paperwork, from a plan agreed in advance with the person. Don't redesign it: its decisions have reasons you may not see.

- **Final review:** started to do it? These instructions don't apply: follow `docs/final-review.md` and change nothing.
- **Sources:** `docs/kit-lite.md` (Brief: what and why; Tasks: the work; Progress log: what has happened) and evidence files the Brief names; not earlier chats or Claude's memory.
- **How you work:** you coordinate and workers build, so your chat stays small and the build runs without the person. On start or "Continue the build", read the Progress log and carry on. Give each task to a fresh worker subagent, never a fork, with a stand-alone brief: the task and its check, the Brief parts it needs, Saving work, the stop list, what to return. Do a task too small to grow your chat (a one-file change) yourself. Run the check yourself, record the evidence, save. Replace a worker that is done, runs long or fails twice with a fresh one, told what was tried; if that fails, park the task, log both attempts, continue, and raise it at the next checkpoint with a recommended answer. Chat long, or just compacted? Write the handoff note and carry on; only if you can't, ask the person to type `/clear` and send "Continue the build". Started as a worker? Do your task only and return what the brief asks.
- **Evidence:** a task is done only when its check passes and the log records a pointer plus one line of result. A check that can only pass later (a scheduled run) becomes a dated checkpoint item, not a blocker. Never weaken a check to pass it.
- **Saving work:** a local git repository, one commit per task (D-3). Work is saved after every task and any change can be undone. Secrets never go into files or history. Commits carry no AI credit lines ("Co-Authored-By: Claude", "Generated with Claude Code").
- **Project settings:** the first task writes `.claude/settings.json` exactly as the Brief says, and its check compares them; change it only with approval.
- **Stop and wait for the person only:** at a checkpoint (a few steps to try and what they should see); before anything on the never list; for their own steps (click by click, with what to paste back); for a change touching a one-way door, the scope, cost, security or the intent. Decide the rest, taking the reading that serves the intent, and log it. Save other questions for the next checkpoint and keep working. An unanswered or timed-out question is never approval.
- **How to ask:** at most four questions, each with 2–4 options in all, the recommended one first with its reason, "You decide" among them. Never ask for secrets in the chat; say where to type them.
- **Never without approval:** deleting or overwriting any receipt, adding dependencies, adding scope.
- **Changes:** a change to a one-way door is marked "Sent to Blueprint" in the log, and that task is parked. Apply an amendment from Blueprint to `kit-lite.md` yourself, section by section, keeping the Progress log; never replace the whole file.
- **Compact Instructions:** keep task states, evidence, decisions, failed approaches, saved questions and the next step.
- **Plain words:** name things as the person would ("the name-clash handling"); an ID such as T-2 may follow in brackets, never stand alone.
- **At the end:** offer the final review, by a fresh reviewer: a subagent given `docs/final-review.md`.
